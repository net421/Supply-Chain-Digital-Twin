# LinkedIn Project Section

Supply Chain Digital Twin & Decision Intelligence Platform

Industrial analytics platform integrating:

- Monte Carlo Simulation
- Digital Twins
- Network Science
- Policy Benchmarking
- Intervention Optimization
- Resilience Analytics
- Executive Reporting

Built using Python, Pandas, Numba and NetworkX.

The platform evaluates supply-chain resilience under uncertainty, benchmarks operational policies and identifies high-impact interventions through simulation-driven decision intelligence.
