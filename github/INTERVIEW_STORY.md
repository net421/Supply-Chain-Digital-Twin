# Interview Story

## Problem

Supply chains operate under uncertainty, but traditional dashboards usually describe what happened rather than evaluating what could happen under disruption.

## Approach

I built a modular Supply Chain Digital Twin platform with:

- Synthetic network generation
- Inventory and disruption simulation
- Monte Carlo scenario engine
- Policy benchmarking
- Network criticality analysis
- Intervention optimization
- Executive reporting

## Technical components

- `generate_network.py`
- `digital_twin.py`
- `monte_carlo.py`
- `numba_core.py`
- `network_criticality.py`
- `intervention_engine.py`
- `executive_report.py`
- `dashboard.py`

## Outcome

The project produced an end-to-end analytics platform spanning Business Analytics, Analytics Engineering, Decision Science, Digital Twins, Network Science and Resilience Analytics.

## Business value

It transforms simulation outputs into executive dashboards, risk prioritization and actionable intervention recommendations.
