# CV Project Section

## Supply Chain Digital Twin & Decision Intelligence Platform
Independent Industrial Analytics Project | 2026

Designed and implemented a Numba-accelerated supply-chain analytics platform integrating synthetic network generation, Monte Carlo simulation, policy benchmarking, network criticality analysis, intervention optimization and executive reporting.

### Key responsibilities and deliverables

- Developed modular analytics architecture across generator, simulation, analytics, policies, optimization and reporting layers.
- Built a Digital Twin simulation engine for inventory dynamics, disruptions, lead-time variability and scenario evaluation.
- Implemented a Monte Carlo experimentation framework for resilience and cost-performance assessment.
- Created network criticality and fragility analytics to identify high-risk operational nodes.
- Developed an intervention optimization engine for resilience investment prioritization.
- Produced executive dashboards and automated reporting outputs for decision support.
- Built six portfolio dashboards demonstrating executive analytics, decision intelligence, digital twin monitoring, network science and resilience analytics.

### Technologies

Python, Pandas, NumPy, Numba, NetworkX, Matplotlib, Monte Carlo Simulation, Digital Twins, Operations Research, Supply Chain Analytics, Decision Intelligence.
